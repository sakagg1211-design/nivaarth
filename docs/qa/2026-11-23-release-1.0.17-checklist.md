# Release 1.0.17+18: review checklist (23 Nov 2026)

What is new since the last APK (1.0.16+17, 20 Nov), and what to check.
Check on both the phone (APK) and the web beta. Do it once in English and once in Hinglish, and once in Dark and once in Light.
Best time: Monday after 9:15 AM, when the market is open. The weekend only shows Friday's closing prices.

## 1. News: "Aaj kyun hila"
- [ ] Stock Detail (for example TITAN) has a news block. It shows today's move against Nifty 50 and against the stock's sector, a short summary, and headlines with their source.
- [ ] Tapping a headline opens the original article.
- [ ] There are no "good/bad" labels. The block only states facts.
- [ ] Dashboard has an "Aapki holdings ki khabrein" card. With Privacy Mode (eye icon) on, the amounts are hidden.
- [ ] In "Aaj ki baatein", a stock that has news today shows a line like "1 news" under it.
- [ ] The Nifty 50 gainers/losers card names its session: "Live" during the market, otherwise "20 Nov close".

## 2. QA fixes (closed market and small errors)
- [ ] On a weekend or holiday the app does not say "Today". It shows the session date ("closing prices of 20 Nov").
- [ ] The LIVE badge and "NSE Real-time" appear only while NSE is open.
- [ ] Stock Detail → Company Overview shows the real sector and industry, not "General".
- [ ] The thesis card appears only when you have written a thesis yourself.
- [ ] The floating + button is gone. Add Holding still works from the + in the header.
- [ ] Top Holdings sits below "Aaj ki baatein".
- [ ] Notifications open without errors. The profile avatar letter is visible.
- [ ] On a split day, the facts and the P&L at the top are correct.

## 3. Light / Dark theme (new)
- [ ] Profile (the "i" icon) → Theme: Dark / Light / Phone.
- [ ] Choose Light. Every screen should turn light: Dashboard, Portfolio, Stock Detail, Advisor, Search, Notifications.
- [ ] After closing and reopening the app, the chosen theme is still there.
- [ ] Phone: the app follows the phone's own dark mode setting.
- [ ] Text on red and green buttons, badges and snackbars is white and readable (Remove, Delete, Import, the bell badge).
- [ ] The first letter in the Top Holdings and Search avatars is visible.
- [ ] Charts (1D/1Y, radar, allocation donut, heatmap) are clear in both themes.
- Known: changing the theme restarts the app at the splash screen, and data takes a few seconds to reload.

## 4. Less gold
- [ ] Gold now appears only on the health ring, the main buttons (+ add, Advisor send) and the login screen.

## 5. TalkBack (accessibility)
- [ ] With TalkBack on, the stock price chart is read out ("Price chart, 1D: … to …, …%").

## 6. Backend (live on Render since 23 Nov)
- [ ] Holdings news loads without a 500 error.
- [ ] The Advisor answer uses the official closing price, shows losses like "-₹…", and ends with the plain disclaimer.
- Seen on 23 Nov, small: amounts come out as "-₹1,792.0" (an extra ".0"), and the answer calls "BELOW HURDLE" a "recommendation". Fix later.

## 7. Always check
- [ ] Login, Add holding, Trade jodo, tradebook import.
- [ ] The phone launcher shows the name "Nivaarth". The version is 1.0.17+18.
- [ ] Monday: NIFTY BANK and NIFTY IT cards show values, the 1D graph runs, and the news matches the right stock.
